AFTERCARE ADVICE
SAMPLE PATIENT AFTERCARE ADVICE 
You may have some discomfort, swelling, redness, peeling of the skin, and bruising around the treatment area(s);
this should appear within a few days.
If you notice any areas of skin that become blistered, broken, pale or grey in colour, or if you are experiencing
ongoing and worsening pain or swelling, you should seek immediate advice. This could be a sign of an adverse
reaction to the treatment.
FOR 24 HOURS
           No touching or rubbing of the skin (this includes facials and facial massage)
           No makeup application
           No excessive exercise or alcohol
           No saunas, steam rooms or swimming pools
           Use fresh, clean pillowcases
           Clean your mobile phone
           Use fresh, clean towels.
FOR 7 DAYS
            No exfoliating skin actives such as acid-based cosmetic products, retinol and scrubs.
FOR 2 WEEKS
           No botulinum toxin treatments
           No prescription skin actives such as benzoyl peroxide, retinoids or topical medications
           No fake tan.
FOR 4 WEEKS
           No filler treatments
           No laser treatments
           No extreme sun exposure.
CONTINUE
          Gentle cleanser
          Moisturiser 
          Tyrosinase inhibitors
          SPF
