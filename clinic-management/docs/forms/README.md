# Consultation forms

Source template: `consultation-consent-aftercare.pdf` (Harley Academy sample — reference only).

Customer iPad journey covers the patient-facing sections:

1. Personal details (+ GP / emergency)
2. Skin concerns & goals
3. Skin health history
4. Skincare routine
5. Skin type + Fitzpatrick
6. Sun, lifestyle & aesthetic history
7. Medical history & precautions
8. Optional clinical photos
9. Consent statements + typed signature
10. Aftercare acknowledgement
11. Optional add-ons

Clinician examination, treatment planning, and treatment-record pages remain staff-only (not on the iPad customer flow).
